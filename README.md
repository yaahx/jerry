# 🔥 uglydogsarecute - Personal Music & Merch Website

An independent artist website featuring a **Burnt Orange** aesthetic, built-in persistent audio player, merchandise shop with cart & checkout inquiries, announcements/news feed, guest messaging, **Admin Control Center**, and real-time **Visitor & Guest Activity Tracking**.

---

## 🎨 Theme: Burnt Orange
- Rich burnt orange (`#f36c21` / `#d9531e`), warm amber highlights (`#ff9f43`), and warm charcoal obsidian backdrops.
- Glowing vinyl center disc, audio player progress bar, and canvas audio spectrum visualizer in burnt orange gradients.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎧 1. Music & Releases
- **Persistent Bottom Player**: Continuous playback while browsing the site, featuring progress scrubbing, volume controls, and an animated audio spectrum visualizer.
- **Interactive Vinyl Showcase**: 3D spinning vinyl record animation tied to active playback.
- **Audio Engine**: Plays uploaded MP3/WAV audio or utilizes a built-in algorithmic synthesizer for immediate playback preview.
- **Genre Filtering**: Easily sort tracks by Burnt Beats, Trap, Lo-Fi, Ambient, or custom genres.

### 🛍️ 2. Store & Merchandise
- **Sell Physical & Digital Items**: Vinyl records, apparel (hoodies, tees), sample packs, beats, or accessories.
- **Slide-out Cart Drawer**: Guest visitors can add items, update quantities, view real-time subtotal, and submit order requests.
- **Order Inquiries**: Directly routes to the artist's admin inbox with guest contact and shipping information.

### 📢 3. News & Updates
- Publish tour dates, studio stories, album pre-order alerts, and general announcements directly to your audience.

### 🔒 4. Admin Control (You vs. Guest Access)
- **Guest Mode**: Visitors can stream tracks, browse merch, add to cart, read announcements, and submit messages/orders.
- **Admin Mode**:
  - Click **Admin Portal** in the navigation bar.
  - Enter Master PIN (Default: `1234`, customizable in Settings).
  - Floating **Edit** and **Delete** buttons appear on every track, product, and post.
  - Dedicated **+ Upload Track**, **+ Add Product**, and **+ Post Update** buttons appear in each section.

### 📊 5. Activity Tracking & Analytics
- **General Statistics**: Total Visitors, Pageviews, Track Streams, Merch Views, Cart Inquiries, and Messages Received.
- **Individual Guest Activity Log**: Real-time event log of visitor sessions (e.g. `Guest #4192 played "Neon Doberman"`, `Guest #8812 added "Burnt Orange Hoodie" to cart`).
- **Export to CSV**: Download visitor logs anytime as a spreadsheet.
